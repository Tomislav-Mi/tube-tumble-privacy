Tube Tumble Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

Tube Tumble is a puzzle game for Android made by Hackney Hackers Ltd ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. The short version: the game has no ads, no accounts of its own and no tracking, and we don't collect your personal information. The only thing you can buy is a one-time unlock for the full game, and Google Play handles that payment.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (which levels you've finished, your sunflower seeds and best moves), your hint balance, your settings, whether you've unlocked the full game and when the game last asked for a review are saved only on your device. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Google Play Games achievements

Tube Tumble uses Google Play Games Services for achievements. If you're signed in to Play Games on your phone, the game reports the achievements you earn to Google. Google also handles your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar) and collects some diagnostic and usage data about the Play Games service. This is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by us, and we can't use it to identify you. You can choose who sees your Play Games activity, or delete it, in the Play Games app or in your Google Account. If you don't use Play Games, the game works exactly the same without it.

Buying the full game

The first two worlds are free. Worlds 3 to 6 can be unlocked with a one-time purchase through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the whole payment with the payment method in your Google Account, under the Google Privacy Policy, and we never see your card or bank details. Google tells the game on your phone that the purchase went through, and the game remembers it on your device. Google keeps the purchase in your order history, which is how Restore purchase brings it back on a new phone.

To process sales, refunds and tax, Google gives us order records: the order number, what was bought, the price, the date, and where in the world it was bought (the country, and for tax sometimes the region or postcode). We use these only to run the game's sales, and we keep them as long as tax law requires.

Ratings and reviews

The first time you clear a world, the game may ask Google Play to show its rating card. Google decides whether it appears, and the game is never told whether you rated it or what you wrote. A rating or review you leave is posted on Google Play under your Google profile, like any other review, and you can edit or delete it there at any time.

Google Play

Like every app on Google Play, Google may give us anonymous crash reports and statistics (such as the number of installs and which Android versions are used). We use them only to fix problems and improve the game.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
  • We don't show ads, and we don't use the advertising ID.
  • We don't sell or share any information with anyone.

Delete your data

Tube Tumble by Hackney Hackers Ltd keeps no data about you on our side apart from the order records described above, which tax law requires us to keep. To remove everything the game has stored:

  1. Progress on your phone: open Android Settings, then Apps, then Tube Tumble, then Storage, and tap Clear storage (or just uninstall the game). This deletes your levels, seeds, hints and settings straight away. A full game you bought isn't lost: tap Restore purchase on the unlock screen to get it back.
  2. Play Games achievements: open the Google Play Games app, go to your profile, then Settings, and choose to delete your Play Games profile or this game's data. You can also manage it in your Google Account. Google deletes this data as described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  3. Purchases and reviews: your order history and any review you've written are part of your Google Account. You can see your orders in the Play Store under your profile, then Payments & subscriptions, and edit or delete a review on the game's Play Store page.

If you'd like help, email us at the address below and we'll walk you through it.
